System design, module boundaries, data flow. How the pieces connect and why.

Active, validated, and out-of-scope requirements. Review the out-of-scope list carefully — these are constraints the AI will respect.
📍 STATE.md
GSD's current position — which phase, what's next, velocity metrics. The AI's persistent memory of where you are.
